## Why this exists

The App Store's terms are widely understood to conflict with the GNU GPL v3's
prohibition on further restrictions. The copyright holder of any GPL program
can resolve this for their own code by granting permission. Because Q Helper
accepts outside contributions, **every contributor must explicitly license their
contributions under the GNU GPL v3 plus this exception** — DCO sign-off alone
does not grant it. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
