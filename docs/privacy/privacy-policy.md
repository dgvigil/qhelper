# Q Helper privacy policy

> **DRAFT. Needs maintainer and legal review before it is published or linked
> from a store listing.** It describes what the app does today. Update it
> whenever behavior changes.

*Effective date: to be set at release.*

Q Helper is a free, open-source app for workout leaders, developed and
published by GV5 Technologies LLC. It is an independent community project and
is not affiliated with or endorsed by F3 Nation.

## What Q Helper collects

**Nothing is sent to GV5 Technologies LLC.** Q Helper has no accounts, no
sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Workouts and
exercise lookup work with no internet connection.

The optional on-device assistant (a prototype, not part of a store release) is
the one exception, and only after the Q chooses Download:

- The phone downloads one model file, about 2.2 GB, from Hugging Face, and
  only on Wi-Fi. That download reaches Hugging Face's servers. No account is
  used. Workouts, names, and questions are not uploaded.
- Questions are answered on the phone from the exercise list stored on the
  phone.
- Delete download removes the model file.

## What is stored on your device

- Your workouts, including titles, segments, activities, and notes.
- Your workout history (the workout you are running or have run).
- Your settings, such as default workout length and whether sounds and
  vibration are on.

This data stays on your phone. It is removed when you delete the app. Q Helper
does not back it up anywhere itself; your phone's own backup (for example
iCloud or Google backup) may include app data according to your phone settings.

## Permissions

- **Notifications (optional).** If you allow them, Q Helper schedules one local
  notification on your phone for when the planned workout time ends. Nothing is
  sent over the internet. If you decline, the app works the same way.
- **Keep the screen on.** Q Helper can keep the screen awake while you lead a
  workout. This is a setting you can turn off. It needs no permission prompt.

Q Helper does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or
microphone.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, Q Helper hands the workout text to your phone's share
sheet. What happens next depends on the app you choose. Q Helper only shares
when you ask it to.

## Children

Q Helper is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the project
repository at `docs/privacy/privacy-policy.md` and the effective date updated.
